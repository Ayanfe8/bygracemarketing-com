# Pre-publish check of the whole site

A check of every page and form before you publish. Nothing gets published as part of this.

## What gets checked
1. **Homepage:** every section loads on phone and desktop (services, packages, pricing comparison, testimonials, FAQ, footer with hello@bygracemarketing.com). Every button and link goes where it should, including Book a call, WhatsApp, the Calendly link and the rate-card downloads.
2. **Starter Pack landing page:** your logo, the approved copy, the workbook covers, the form (required fields, the optional business details, and the two marketing checkboxes unticked by default), and the footer on phone and desktop.
3. **Starter Pack thank-you, privacy and unsubscribe pages:** they load correctly and both workbook downloads open.
4. **DFY vs DWY guide page:** it loads and has its search details.
5. **Forms, tested end to end:** one Starter Pack request and one consultation request. Each is checked for:
   - being saved,
   - showing up in the DFY LEADS sheet,
   - sending the email from hello@.

   I'll remove the test entries from the backend and the sheet afterwards.
6. **Technical health:** any site errors, the security scan, page titles and descriptions, the sitemap and robots file.

## What you get back
A short pass/fail list. Small problems get fixed straight away. Anything that needs your decision gets flagged so you can choose.
