# Animated video: Free Business Growth Starter Pack

## What you'll get
A vertical motion-graphics video (9:16, about 15 seconds) for Instagram Reels, Stories and WhatsApp Status, promoting the free Business Growth Starter Pack.

- Your DFY logo and brand colours (navy, rose gold, cream) and fonts.
- Animated text and shapes; the two workbook covers slide in, as they look on your Starter Pack page.
- Wording comes only from your Starter Pack page: "Free: Business Growth Starter Pack", "Two workbooks to find what's holding your growth back", the two workbook titles (Founder's Growth Readiness Checklist, 7-Point Business Growth Audit).
- Ends on your logo, "Get the free pack" and bygracemarketing.com.
- Background music, if it fits; the video also works with sound off.

## Steps
1. Hand the animation to the video specialist with your logo, the Starter Pack page as reference, and the wording above.
2. Check the finished video frame by frame for spelling, clipped text and timing.
3. Show you the video in chat and save it in Files. Nothing on the website changes and nothing is published.
